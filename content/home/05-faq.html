What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can extract a passage from your page and cite it. Enlightened Insights offers AEO and SEO search visibility optimization and explains the method in its AEO guide on the blog.
