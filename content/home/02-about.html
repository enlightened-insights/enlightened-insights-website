What is Enlightened Insights?

Enlightened Insights is a Toronto-based consultancy that builds marketing data systems and AI automation, and optimizes brands' visibility in search engines and AI answers (SEO and AEO).

Enlightened Insights connects the tools marketing teams already use, such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Google Analytics, into dashboards and pipelines the whole team can trust, then automates the repetitive work around them.

For a Shopify ecommerce client, Enlightened Insights grew organic search clicks by 17.7% in five weeks. Read the case study, or see how answer engine optimization works.
